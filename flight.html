Schwab Lab · Flight Lab 2.0 — an educational post-flight analysis tool. Flight data is processed locally; the optional map mode loads Leaflet and map tiles from third parties. Simplified example uses gravity, linear velocity damping, horizontal wind, and an illustrative high-drag descent phase. Not flight-certified or suitable for planning, guidance, targeting, recovery timing, or controlling physical vehicles. .
